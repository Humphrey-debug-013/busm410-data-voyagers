# Project Audit

Read [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), [Other/audit_protocol.md](../../../Other/audit_protocol.md), and [Other/audit_log.md](../../../Other/audit_log.md) before maintaining audit records.

## Protocol

The team's protocol is currently a CP0 placeholder. Do not describe the instructor's process as an established team practice. Propose substantive process additions for the team to agree on before presenting them as adopted.

## Log

Record meaningful human checks of AI work: source verification, schema or calculation checks, challenged assumptions, and corrected interpretations. Skip routine approvals, formatting requests, and the agent's own checks.

Use the existing log columns: Date, Reviewer, Item checked, Method or evidence, Result, Follow-up. Preserve existing entries and avoid duplicates. Do not invent reviews, reviewers, results, dates, or sources. Mark unresolved issues pending and distinguish a reported check from independently available evidence.

This guidance does not authorize data changes, query execution, spending, publishing, or unrelated edits.
